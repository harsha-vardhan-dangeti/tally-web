Privacy

Last updated 25 October 2026

You can use Tally in two ways. Without an account, nothing you log leaves your browser. If you sign in with Google, Tally also keeps your records in its database so your devices stay in step. You choose, and you can delete everything at any time.

Without an account

  • Your expenses, income, repeats, budgets, bills, accounts, goals and settings are saved in your browser's database (IndexedDB) on your device.
  • Nothing you log is sent to any server.
  • Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes it, so back up from Settings now and then.

Signed in with Google

  • What's stored: the same records as above, plus your name, email address and Google profile photo, which Google shares when you sign in. Tally doesn't ask Google for anything else.
  • Where: in Tally's database, run by Supabase on servers in the United States (AWS us-east-1). Your device also keeps its own copy, so Tally works offline and uploads changes when you're back online.
  • Who can see it: only your account can read or change your records; other Tally users can't. The person who runs Tally can access the database to keep it working, as with any online service.
  • Signing out removes Tally's data from that browser. It stays in your account.

Deleting your data

  • Delete account (Settings → Account) permanently deletes your account and everything synced to it, signs out your other devices, and clears this browser. Download a backup first if you want to keep your records.
  • Delete all data (Settings) clears your records. When you're signed in, that includes the copy in your account.

Services Tally uses

  • Vercel hosts the website and app, and may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and time) for security and reliability.
  • Supabase stores signed-in people's records and handles sign-in.
  • Google handles "Sign in with Google".
  • jsDelivr serves the receipt reader the first time you scan a receipt. The photo is read on your device and isn't uploaded.

Tally has no ads, analytics or third-party trackers, and never sells or shares your data.

App lock

The Face ID / Touch ID lock uses your device's passkey sign-in to hide the app until you unlock it. It's a privacy screen: it doesn't encrypt the data stored in your browser.

Backups and imports

Backups are JSON files saved to your device; anyone with the file can read it. Bank statements you import (PDF, Excel or CSV) are read in your browser and never uploaded; a statement's password is used only to open it and isn't stored. Only the transactions you choose to add are saved.
