    Your data, your choice

    Without an account, nothing you log leaves your browser. Sign in with Google and Tally keeps a copy in your account, so your phone and laptop show the same numbers.

    Without an account
    Everything stays in this browser's own database (IndexedDB) on your device. Nothing you log is sent anywhere.
    Signed in with Google
    Your records are also stored in Tally's database (Supabase, in the United States). Other Tally users can't see them. Your device keeps its own copy, so Tally still works offline.
    Deleting it
    Settings → Delete account removes your account and everything synced to it. Delete all data clears what's stored.
    Receipt photos
    Read on your device. The receipt reader itself is downloaded from jsDelivr the first time you scan one; the photo isn't uploaded.
    When Tally updates
    A new version downloads in the background and Tally shows Update ready. Reloading swaps the app's files; your expenses aren't touched.
    Backups
    One tap saves a JSON file you keep. Restoring checks the file before anything changes, and you can put your earlier data back afterwards.
    The trade-off
    Without an account, clearing this site's data in your browser deletes your expenses, so Tally reminds you to back up every 30 days.

    Put it one tap away

    iPhone

    Open Tally in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen. If you'll log from Shortcuts, turn off Open as Web App so both use Safari's data.

    Android

    Open Tally in Chrome, then menu → Install app. Long-press the icon for Quick add and Smart add.

    Computer

    Install it from the address bar, or keep a tab open. Press N to log and / to search.
